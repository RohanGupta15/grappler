# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is not a replacement for the full text.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@RohanGupta15](https://github.com/RohanGupta15), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
